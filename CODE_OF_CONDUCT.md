# Code of conduct

faztudo follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, criticize ideas and not people, and
make room for newcomers. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not
accepted in issues, pull requests, chats or any other project space.

Report problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub (the email on the
maintainer's profile). Reports are handled confidentially, and the
maintainer may warn, suspend or ban anyone who breaks these rules.
